《东溪岭遗赠》是一款合作式黑暗侦探剧情游戏。玩家走进一座衰败的矿业小镇，在五个彼此关联的篇章中追查超自然谜团，逐步拼出隐藏在镇上的故事。

### 连续篇章中的调查

调查贯穿整段冒险。玩家共同梳理发现、讨论线索，在阴郁的环境与不断浮现的秘密中推进故事，体验一场需要桌边交流的叙事调查。

<!-- @en -->

*Legacy of Eastbrook Hills* is a cooperative dark detective game set in a decaying mining town. Across five connected episodes, players investigate supernatural mysteries and piece together the story hidden within the town.

### An investigation across connected episodes

The mystery runs through the entire adventure. Players discuss their discoveries and work through the clues together as new secrets emerge, making conversation at the table part of the investigation.
