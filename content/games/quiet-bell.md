《空寂之钟》是一款洛夫克拉夫特风格的冒险侦探游戏。互动式分支叙事与非线性人物交流，让调查沿着不同方向展开。

### 透明卡牌的调查体验

游戏将独特的透明卡牌调查机制融入冒险，让实体卡牌成为追索秘密的一部分。随着对人物和线索的了解加深，玩家逐步走近故事中的真相。

*Quiet Bell* is a Lovecraftian adventure detective game. An interactive branching narrative and nonlinear character interactions allow the investigation to unfold in different directions.

### Investigation through transparent cards

A distinctive transparent-card investigation mechanic makes the physical cards part of uncovering the mystery. Players learn more about the characters and clues as they work towards the truth behind the story.
