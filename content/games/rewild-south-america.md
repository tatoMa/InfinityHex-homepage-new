《归野南美》是一款围绕南美洲多样生态系统展开的卡牌驱动策略游戏。玩家通过卡牌选择与引擎构筑，逐步恢复和发展自己的生态系统。

### 让生态成为策略

自然主题贯穿游戏的发展过程。如何安排卡牌、建立效果之间的联系，并让自己的生态持续成长，是这场策略体验的核心。

### 中文版进度

简体中文版已完成众筹，目前处于生产阶段。

<!-- @en -->

*Rewild: South America* is a card-driven strategy game about restoring and developing South America’s diverse ecosystems. Players build an engine through their card choices as their ecosystems grow.

### Ecology as strategy

The natural theme runs through the game’s development. Choosing cards and building connections between their effects shapes how each ecosystem develops over time.

### Chinese edition

The Simplified Chinese edition has completed crowdfunding and is in production.
