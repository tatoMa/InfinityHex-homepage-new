*Boktor* is a fantasy adventure game built around exploration, encounters and character-driven play. Players journey through a distinctive world, with their heroes at the centre of the experience.

### Exploration and character-driven play

Exploring new areas, responding to encounters and making use of character abilities shape the adventure as players press deeper into the world.

### Chinese edition

The Simplified Chinese edition has completed crowdfunding and is in production.
