《Chroma Arcana》是一款以法师对决为中心的奇幻战术卡牌游戏。玩家运用魔法展开竞争，体验围绕卡牌与战术选择形成的正面较量。

### 对决与合作

游戏既包含法师之间的竞技战斗，也提供共同挑战强大敌人的合作内容。合作挑战属于扩展内容，具体可玩模式以对应版本所含组件为准。

### 中文版进度

简体中文版已完成众筹，目前处于生产阶段。

<!-- @en -->

*Chroma Arcana* is a tactical fantasy card game centred on duelling mages. Players wield magic in competitive battles shaped by card play and tactical decisions.

### Duels and cooperative challenges

Alongside competitive mage battles, cooperative expansion content offers challenges against powerful enemies. Available modes depend on the components included in the relevant edition.

### Chinese edition

The Simplified Chinese edition has completed crowdfunding and is in production.
