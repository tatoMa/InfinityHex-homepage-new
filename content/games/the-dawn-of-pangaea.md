《盘古黎明》是一款以远古盘古大陆为舞台的策略游戏。玩家通过板块放置与引擎构筑，塑造大地景观，发展生态系统。

### 在版图上规划成长

游戏将空间布局与持续发展结合起来。板块如何落位、效果如何衔接，决定了玩家在这片古老大陆上的发展方向。

### 中文版进度

简体中文版已完成众筹，目前处于生产阶段。

<!-- @en -->

*The Dawn of Pangaea* is a strategy game about shaping the landscapes and ecosystems of the ancient supercontinent. Tile placement and engine building work together as players develop the land.

### Planning growth across the landscape

The game connects spatial planning with ongoing development. Where tiles are placed and how their effects work together shape each player’s approach to this ancient world.

### Chinese edition

The Simplified Chinese edition has completed crowdfunding and is in production.
