《Pirates of the High Teas》将海盗冒险搬上下午茶桌，是一款充满奇趣的策略游戏。茶会、走私、糕点和食物大战，共同组成这个热闹又独特的海盗世界。

### 不太规矩的茶会

精致茶点遇上海盗作风，日常的下午茶也成了较量的舞台。游戏以轻松诙谐的主题呈现策略互动，让桌边的竞争多一份玩笑与热闹。

### 中文版进度

简体中文版已完成众筹，目前处于生产阶段。

<!-- @en -->

*Pirates of the High Teas* is a playful strategy game that brings piracy to the afternoon tea table. Tea parties, smuggling, pastries and food fights make for an unusually mischievous setting.

### A rather unruly tea party

Refined refreshments meet pirate behaviour, turning afternoon tea into a lively contest. The whimsical theme gives the strategic interaction a light-hearted setting.

### Chinese edition

The Simplified Chinese edition has completed crowdfunding and is in production.
