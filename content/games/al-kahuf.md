*Al-Kahuf* is an investigation-focused narrative game built around solving mysteries, making discoveries and piecing together a larger story.

### From discoveries to understanding

Investigation and deduction drive the experience. As players gather information, individual discoveries become part of the broader narrative, inviting them to follow clues and connect events.
