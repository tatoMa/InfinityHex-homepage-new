《永夜光墟》（S.H.E.O.L. 2.0）是一款黑暗科幻题材的合作战役游戏。玩家围绕人类的生存展开行动，在战术战斗与探索中推进战役。

### 生存之战

探索、成长与战斗紧密相连。玩家在旅程中逐步发展自身能力，迎接危机，共同面对这个科幻世界带来的生存挑战。

### 中文版进度

已签署本地化协议，简体中文版以《永夜光墟》为名推出。众筹与发行尚待后续推进。

*S.H.E.O.L. 2.0* is a cooperative campaign game set in a dark science-fiction world. Players fight for humanity’s survival through tactical combat and exploration.

### A campaign for survival

Exploration, progression and combat are closely connected. Players develop their capabilities as the campaign unfolds and face the threats to humanity together.

### Chinese edition

The localisation agreement has been signed. Crowdfunding and release are still to follow.
