《文明纪元》（Epochs: Course of Cultures）是一款跨越多个时代的文明策略游戏。玩家通过扩张、研究、贸易与治理，推动自己的文明向前发展。

### 文明的不同道路

战争与科技发展同样影响文明的进程。如何分配精力、选择发展方向，并应对时代中的变化，是玩家需要持续权衡的问题。

*Epochs: Course of Cultures* is a civilization strategy game spanning multiple eras. Players guide their society through expansion, research, trade and government.

### Different paths through history

Warfare and technological development also shape a civilization’s course. Players balance their priorities, choose a direction and respond to changing circumstances as the eras unfold.
